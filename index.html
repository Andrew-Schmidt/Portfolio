/ ABOUT

Field-tested thinking.
Builder's mindset.

My day-to-day work focuses on residential and business broadband drop systems, from the tap to customer premises equipment. I use Viavi OneExpert, leakage detection, modem diagnostics and Wi-Fi analysis to investigate service issues.

Outside of work, I build tools and run technical labs because I enjoy understanding how systems function beyond the surface. My projects connect broadband fundamentals with networking, Linux, programming, and hardware integration.
