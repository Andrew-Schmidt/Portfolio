Why I built it

Getting a stream from an encoder to receiving devices required diagnosing protocol configuration, routing and firewall issues.

My approach

Configured OBS publishing to MediaMTX and worked through stream path errors, network segmentation, TCP connectivity, firewall rules and remote playback. Also developed setup documentation.
